## Two implementation notes that matter

1. **A session is not a calendar day.** The unit runs three shifts and the last crosses midnight.
   Keying sessions on the calendar date splits them in two — inflating the count by ~44% and
   silently destroying the weight fields. Sessions are cut as contiguous runs of readings
   separated by more than six hours, and dated to the dialysis day beginning at 08:00.
2. **`uf` is an ultrafiltration rate (L/h)**, matching the published summary statistics
   (0.52 +/- 0.41); it is the control variable, and clinicians do vary it mid-session.
